And The Time Is — Visual Direction Wireframes

Ten visual directions for evaluation: four Horizon skins (A–D) on the globe-and-ring foundation, plus six radical concepts (1–6) that leave the observatory metaphor behind.

Note: Wireframes for evaluation — not production code. Open each option in a new tab to compare layouts side-by-side.

Radical Concepts — Beyond Globe & Ring

Alternative interaction models — no rotating ring, no wireframe globe.

🎼
Concept 1
The Conductor

Timeline mixer — horizontal lanes per timezone, scrubber as crossfader, overlap windows as harmonic beats.

Concept 2
The Relay

Transit map — zones as stations on interconnected lines, handoffs and transfer windows instead of orbital positions.

View wireframe →

Concept 3
The Threshold

Door corridor — each timezone is a doorway with light spilling through; walk the hall to compare moments across zones.

Concept 4
The Pulse

Relationship graph — people and places as nodes, time drift as edge tension, meeting windows as pulsing links.

Concept 5
The Ledger

Schedule-first — dense tabular day view, timezone columns as accounts, availability as debits and credits.

The Pass

Alto-inspired parallax ride — scrub to snowboard through the day, landmarks as cities, birds, tricks, aurora overlap.
